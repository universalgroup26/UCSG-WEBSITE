## What UCSG Does

UCSG is an education consulting firm that helps international students:

1. **Day 1 CPT University Placement** — Connect students to SEVP-certified U.S. universities offering Day 1 CPT (Curricular Practical Training) programs, allowing off-campus work from day one of enrollment.
2. **University Transfers** — Handle SEVIS record transfer, new I-20 issuance, and credit evaluation for seamless university-to-university transfers while maintaining F-1 visa status.
3. **SEVIS Reinstatement** — Guide students whose SEVIS records have been terminated through the reinstatement application process.
4. **Change of Status** — Assist international students with visa status changes (e.g., B-1/B-2 to F-1).
5. **STEM OPT Support** — Help students navigate the STEM OPT extension process for 24 additional months of work authorization.
6. **Scholarship Guidance** — Identify and apply for scholarships available to international students.

## Key Services in Detail

### Day 1 CPT
Day 1 CPT allows F-1 students to start working immediately upon enrollment. UCSG matches students with universities whose programs authorize CPT from the first day. Popular programs include MBA, MS Computer Science, MS Data Science, MS Cybersecurity, and more.

### University Transfers
For students who need to change universities (due to SEVIS issues, program quality, or career goals), UCSG manages the entire transfer including credit evaluation, SEVIS transfer, and new I-20 processing.

### SEVIS Reinstatement
If a student's SEVIS record is terminated, UCSG provides expert guidance on filing Form I-539 for reinstatement, preparing the application package, and communicating with USCIS.

### Change of Status
Students currently in the U.S. on other visa types (B-1/B-2, H-4, etc.) can change to F-1 status with UCSG's guidance. This includes filing the I-539 application and preparing supporting documentation.
