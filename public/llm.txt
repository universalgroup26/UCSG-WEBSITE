## What UCSG Does

UCSG is an education consulting firm that helps international students:

1. **CPT University Placement** — Connect students to SEVP-certified U.S. universities offering CPT (Curricular Practical Training) programs, allowing off-campus work related to their field of study.
2. **University Transfers** — Guide students through SEVIS record transfer, new I-20 issuance, and credit evaluation for university-to-university transfers while maintaining F-1 visa status.
3. **SEVIS Reinstatement** — Guide students whose SEVIS records have been terminated through the reinstatement application process.
4. **Change of Status** — Assist international students with visa status changes (e.g., B-1/B-2 to F-1).
5. **STEM OPT Support** — Help students explore backup university options and understand the STEM OPT extension process.
